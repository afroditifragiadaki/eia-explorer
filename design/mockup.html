How have residential electricity prices changed in New York vs Florida since 2015?

From January 2015 to June 2026, New York's average residential price rose from 19.3¢ to 29.5¢ per kWh, about 53%. Florida's rose from 11.7¢ to 15.1¢, about 29%.

New York has been more expensive throughout, and the gap has widened from roughly 7.6¢ to 14.4¢ per kWh. Both series are seasonal; New York's winter peaks are the sharpest.
